Why Don't We Feel Earth Moving?

Earth is spinning incredibly fast and travelling around the Sun. So why don't we feel ourselves rushing through space?

🌎 Earth is always moving

It might seem like Earth is completely still beneath our feet, but it is actually in constant motion.

Earth rotates around its axis once approximately every 24 hours. At the same time, Earth travels around the Sun at an average speed of about 30 kilometres per second.

And that's not all. Our entire Solar System is also moving through the Milky Way.

🚀 So why don't we feel it?

The main reason is that we are moving with Earth.

You, the ground beneath you, the atmosphere, oceans, buildings, and almost everything around you are travelling together with Earth.

💡 Motion itself isn't something we automatically feel. What we usually notice is a change in motion, such as speeding up, slowing down, or changing direction.

🚌 Think about sitting in a smooth bus

Imagine you are sitting inside a bus travelling at a constant speed on a straight, smooth road.

If you close your eyes, you may not be able to tell how fast the bus is moving. You and everything inside the bus are moving together.

But if the driver suddenly brakes or accelerates, you immediately notice the change.

Earth works in a similar way. Its motion is generally smooth, so we don't experience a constant sensation of movement.

🌬️ What about Earth's atmosphere?

The atmosphere also moves along with Earth because gravity keeps it close to our planet.

That means we aren't constantly being hit by a giant stream of air simply because Earth is rotating and orbiting the Sun.

Wind happens because air moves relative to Earth's surface, mainly because of differences in heating and pressure.

🌀 Earth's rotation

Earth rotates once roughly every 24 hours. The surface moves fastest near the equator and more slowly as you move toward the poles.

Even though this sounds incredibly fast, the rotation is smooth and continuous. There is no sudden change that would make us feel Earth's rotation directly.

☀️ What about Earth's journey around the Sun?

Earth travels around the Sun at an average speed of about 30 kilometres per second.

That's around 108,000 kilometres per hour!

Yet we don't feel ourselves racing through space because Earth and everything around us are travelling together.

🧠 What do we actually feel?

Our bodies are sensitive to forces and changes in movement. For example, you can feel a lift start moving, a car turning, or a roller coaster accelerating.

But if your motion is smooth and nearly constant, your body doesn't have a simple sense that tells you your absolute speed through space.

🔭 Quick Facts

  • Earth rotates once approximately every 24 hours.
  • Earth travels around the Sun at about 30 km/s on average.
  • We move along with Earth and its atmosphere.
  • We usually notice changes in motion more than constant motion.
  • Earth's motion through space is not something our bodies directly sense as a constant feeling.

🌌 So, why don't we feel Earth moving?

Because we are moving with it.

Earth, its atmosphere, and everything on its surface are travelling together. Since Earth's motion is mostly smooth and continuous, we don't experience it as a constant sensation.

So while you may feel perfectly still right now, you're actually travelling through space at astonishing speeds. 🌍✨
